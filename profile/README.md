## Who We Are

**JLM ENSA Khouribga** is the local team of **Jeunes Leaders Marocains (JLM)** at the **École Nationale des Sciences Appliquées de Khouribga**, Université Sultan Moulay Slimane, Morocco.

Part of a Moroccan social entrepreneurship initiative, our club brings students together to turn ideas into projects with social and sustainable impact. We connect engineering skills, community engagement and entrepreneurial thinking to respond to real needs in Khouribga and beyond.

> **All we do is for our nation.**

## Our Mission

We build practical solutions, support local communities and help students become responsible leaders through teamwork and action.

| Our pillars | What they mean in practice |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🔴 **Social entrepreneurship** | Support artisans, service providers, cooperatives and young entrepreneurs with solutions that create lasting value. |
| 🟢 **Solidarity** | Mobilize students around community needs and support vulnerable groups through concrete actions. |
| 🟡 **Environment** | Promote responsible use of water, energy and natural resources. |

**Innovation, education and leadership** support all three pillars.

## Our Projects

| Project | Focus | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HrayfIA** | Craftsmanship · AI · E-commerce | Promote Moroccan artisans through a digital marketplace, intelligent assistance and recommendations. |
| **EduPlan** | Education · AI | Help students organize their learning and receive personalized study support. |
| **Nkhdam Lik** | Local services · Digital inclusion | Connect local service providers with citizens, students, clubs and associations. |
| **EcoWatt** | Energy · IoT · Data | Make electricity consumption visible and help users identify opportunities to reduce waste. |
| **AquaFlow** | Water · IoT · AI | Monitor water consumption and detect leaks early. |

## Our Actions & Programs

| Initiative | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI Days** | Introduce high-school students to artificial intelligence through practical workshops and educational orientation. |
| **Smiley Business Challenge** | Train students to support cooperatives and startups through diagnostics, audits and practical recommendations. |
| **Ofok Tech Khouribga** | Develop digital and entrepreneurial skills among young people. |
| **Caravane Jossor** | Support vulnerable communities through solidarity, medical consultations, donations and community actions. |
| **Miséricorde** | Create moments of connection and support for elderly residents. |

Our club also organizes training sessions, bootcamps, team-building activities and events that strengthen leadership, communication, entrepreneurship and digital skills.

## Our Organization

Our work combines club coordination, project teams and support functions:

- **Leadership & coordination** guide the club's priorities and collective work.
- **Project leaders & multidisciplinary teams** develop and carry out each initiative.
- **Communication, partnerships, training and logistics** support project delivery and community engagement.

Each project brings together students with complementary skills around a shared purpose.

## How We Work

1. **Understand the need** through field observation and conversations with beneficiaries.
2. **Design a useful solution** and define the intended impact.
3. **Build a team** and prepare a prototype or action plan.
4. **Test on the ground** and collect feedback.
5. **Measure, improve and sustain** the project over time.

We focus on **real beneficiaries, measurable impact and sustainable solutions**.

## What Members Gain

- Leadership and real-world project management experience
- Communication, teamwork and self-confidence
- Entrepreneurial thinking and practical digital skills
- Active citizenship and community engagement
- Opportunities to learn, build and represent ENSA Khouribga in JLM activities and competitions
